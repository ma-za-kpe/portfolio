v1.0 Brand guidelines

The maku. brand kit

One idea runs through everything: Africa-built, global-grade. That means confident, precise and warm. The ink is calm, the ember is energetic, and the full stop says the work is finished and shipped.

01Logo

The mark is a single-stroke M followed by an ember full stop. The line is engineering; the dot means shipped, done, decided. Use the app-icon mark for avatars and favicons, the glyph inline beside text, and the wordmark wherever there's room.

Clear space: keep at least the dot's diameter × 2 around the mark. Minimum size: 16 px for the mark and 72 px wide for the wordmark.

02Colour

Lead with ink, punctuate with ember. Volt is for live and status signals only. Iris only ever appears inside gradients. A good rough mix is 70% ink, 20% bone, 8% ember and 2% volt.

04Voice

Short, specific and confident without hype. Every claim needs a number or a product behind it ("receipts, not adjectives"). Lead with the outcome, then the stack.

✓ Do

  • "Cut monthly infra spend 41%."
  • "Offline-first, built for 2G."
  • Pair a bold sans line with one serif-italic twist.
  • End statements with the ember full stop.

✕ Don't

  • "Passionate rockstar ninja developer."
  • Stack more than one accent colour in a single element.
  • Use volt for decoration.
  • Stretch, outline or recolour the mark.
